What is ready, and what comes next

These are separate, original-resolution static PNGs. Characters and icons have real alpha transparency; rooms are opaque full-room plates. All artwork was generated with the built-in image tool, with exact prompts included alongside the files.

  • Six careers, with second Worker and Explorer appearances. Smugglers use their public career art unchanged; seat labels belong in the game UI.
  • Eight room themes share four centered passage positions. Four additional plates show depleted or triggered states. These are flattened images, not layered or pixel-stable tilemaps.
  • Shared-identity directional walk/action/death/ghost animation, eight mild450ms haunt appearances, final scale, occlusion, sealed doorway overlays and collision/path testing still need a production integration pass. These future assets are not included here.
  • The lantern art does not make a Safe Zone. The hook is private inventory art and must never expose a theft animation. Compass needle artwork is decorative, not live guidance.
