**Exact generation prompt:**

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one unified game character design sheet, not a finished animation atlas.
Primary request: design the full eight-player public-career cast for a playful, tense multiplayer underground escape game. Careers must be immediately recognizable by clothing and silhouette.
Style: polished original 2D game concept illustration, chunky compact adult adventurers about three heads tall, bold clean ink silhouettes, simple cel shading with subtle hand-painted texture, expressive friendly faces, functional industrial expedition clothes. Slightly elevated three-quarter front view suitable as a reference for a top-down game. Not photorealistic, not a 3D render, no existing franchise designs.
Composition: landscape sheet with exactly eight full-body characters arranged as two rows of four equally spaced cells, every head and boot fully visible, consistent scale and soft ground shadows. Warm pale parchment background with a restrained dark-ink title at top. Spacious art-book presentation with crisp simple role labels below each character. No extra characters, no scene scenery.
Exact title: "CAREER CAST".
First row from left to right:
1. "WORKER 01": squat square silhouette, mustard-yellow mining hardhat with an unlit round headlamp, orange work overalls over dark undershirt, oversized dark work gloves, broad boots. One stripe on helmet. Broad cheerful face, dark skin.
2. "WORKER 02": same unmistakable career uniform and palette as Worker01, two stripes on helmet, different face and hairstyle peeking out, lighter skin, ponytail. Similar stout silhouette. Not a different profession.
3. "EXPLORER 01": teal short expedition coat, a broad tan field hat with folded goggles on its band, narrow upright rolled-map backpack, dark hiking boots, one small diamond patch. Medium skin, alert curious face.
4. "EXPLORER 02": matching teal expedition coat, same broad tan field hat silhouette with goggles, rolled-map backpack, two small diamond patches, different hair and face, dark skin. Both Explorers look like members of the same profession.
Second row from left to right:
5. "MERCHANT": plum tailored waistcoat and short flared coat, cream high collar, gold trim, distinctive large rectangular crossbody ledger satchel with square clasp, neat hair, confident welcoming expression. Upright silhouette with a flat rectangular bag at hip. No coins spilling.
6. "GUARD": cobalt-blue padded uniform, broad squared ivory shoulder plates, squared open-face cap-helmet with short central ridge, thick forearm guards, shield-shaped chest badge. Stable upright stance. Face visible, medium-brown skin. No handheld shield, no glowing protection aura.
7. "THIEF": slim charcoal outfit, lime-green inner lining on a sharply pointed close-fitting hood, short split coat tails, dark eye mask, soft narrow boots, triangular silhouette, sly lively expression. No scary villain features.
8. "SCAVENGER": rust-red patched utility jacket, olive cargo trousers, rounded padded aviator cap with large round goggles resting on forehead, asymmetrical oversized rounded salvage backpack, reinforced boots, friendly resourceful face. No skulls or gore.
All eight stand in relaxed empty-handed poses. Costume equipment such as belts, satchels and goggles is decorative, not an indicator of available consumable tools. No pickaxes, drills, hooks or guns in hands. Clearly different silhouettes for the six careers, while Worker duplicates and Explorer duplicates retain their shared role look. Characters represent varied people without tying a career to a particular gender or ethnicity.
Constraints: exactly eight figures and the labels above, legible typography, no secret Smuggler character, no secret-role hints or criminal emblem on otherwise ordinary careers, no health bars, no UI chrome, no watermark. This is a career costume exploration sheet, not an in-game screenshot.
