⛏ MINE ESCAPE

A real-time multiplayer game of cooperation and secret greed

👥 You need at least 3 players to start a game (up to 8). Everyone plays on their own phone, tablet or computer — no login and nothing to install. Trying it alone? Open the game in three separate browser tabs or windows; each tab gets its own seat.

▶ Play Mine Escape

🎯 The story and the goal

The mine has collapsed behind your crew. You have 10 rounds to get everyone out. Each player starts with 2 coins and 3 health.

  • Escape together: put coins into the shared escape fund. At the end of any round, if the fund holds 11 coins for every living player, the whole group escapes and wins.
  • Escape by luck: three hidden natural exits lie deep in the mine (14+ rooms away). Discovering one saves every survivor for free.
  • Escape alone: some players are secretly Smugglers (see below).

Anyone still underground after round 10 loses.

🕵 The hidden Smugglers

At the start, 0–2 players are secretly made Smugglers. A Smuggler looks exactly like a normal career and has all its abilities — but wins by holding 25 coins in their own wallet at the end of a round, buying a private escape.

Wallets are private, but every contribution to the fund is public. So the real game is trust: who promised coins and never paid? Who keeps "forgetting" to deposit? You can steal from suspects, fight them, or put them on trial — but you might be wrong. Everyone's secret identity is revealed when the match ends.

👷 Careers

Every player gets a public career. A 3-player team is always a Worker, an Explorer and a Merchant; bigger teams add more.

CareerWhat they do
WorkerMines up to 2 coins per round (everyone else can mine only 1).
ExplorerThe only career that can drill new rooms. Gets a finder's coin from any treasure they uncover.
MerchantSells extra action slots and advanced goods (potions, rescue kits, compasses, reinforced lanterns) and upgrades their shop. Gets a free potion each round.
GuardSells a wallet lock (stops theft) and a life shield (blocks one hit) each round.
ThiefSteals up to 3 coins per robbery instead of 1.
ScavengerLoots three times faster and can take every coin from a fallen player.

⏱ How a round works (130 seconds)

TimeWhat happens
0–10 sArrival — walk to a camp (a bright crystal Safe Zone).
10–50 sCamp choices — fill your action slots, shop, hire the Guard, post contribution pledges.
50–60 sDeparture — head out into the mine.
60–110 sField — mine, drill, loot, trade blows, deposit coins.
110–130 sReturn & settlement — walk back to camp; the escape check happens at the end.

Nobody teleports: you physically walk back to a camp every round, so plan your route. The colored bar at the top of the screen shows where you are in the round.

🎮 How to play

  • Move: arrow keys / WASD, or drag anywhere on a phone screen.
  • Act: everything you can do appears in the "What do you do?" box at the bottom, written in plain words — "⛏ Mine a coin here", "🔨 Drill a tunnel east", "🏦 Deposit all 5 coins". Tap a choice or press its number key. Choices you can't use yet stay visible with the reason, so the game teaches itself.
  • Prepare in camp: you get 2 free action slots each round (up to 4 with the Merchant's help). Fill each with a free tool, an item you own, or a Deposit/Trial marker. A tool you prepare but don't use keeps its slot for next round.
  • Stand still while working: mining, drilling and looting take 3 seconds. Moving or being attacked interrupts you — but you keep your tool and can try again.

✨ Game features

🗺 A mine that grows
Explorers drill rooms that randomly turn out to be coin mines, treasure vaults, supply caches, traps, new camps or empty galleries — and can dig downward for richer mines.
🏦 Shared escape fund
One public goal everyone can see, with private wallets that nobody can.
📜 Pledges
No chat — instead, post a plan asking each player for coins. Others approve or reject, and the board shows who actually paid.
🪝 Silent theft
Rob anyone in the same room at any time. The victim isn't told — they just notice their wallet is lighter.
⚔ Combat & looting
Clubs, blades and guns from the private machine. Fallen players leave their coins behind for looting.
⚖ Trials
Accuse someone in camp. If more than half of all living players vote guilty, they are executed.
👻 Ghosts & haunting
Eliminated players become ghosts that drift through walls and see everyone. Among the living, only their killer can see them — and once a round the ghost can haunt their killer with a ghostly swoop and whoosh.
🗳 Explorer elections
If every Explorer is lost, survivors vote to make a volunteer the new Explorer.
🧭 Compass & natural exits
A pricey compass gives a rough direction to the nearest hidden exit.
🔌 Drop-in reconnects
Lose your connection? Reopen the game in the same browser within a round and you're back in.

🛠 How it's built

  • Plays in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers — nothing to download, no accounts. Players join with a 4-letter room code or an invite link.
  • One game server keeps every screen in sync. It runs all the rules (coins, combat, discoveries, votes) so nobody can cheat by editing their browser.
  • Secrets stay secret. Each player's device only receives what that player is allowed to know — your wallet, items and secret role are never sent to anyone else.
  • Live movement through a 2D cartoon mine, with hand-made room, character and item artwork.
  • Kept simple on purpose: games live in the server's memory (no database), so a server restart ends matches in progress; characters are static illustrations rather than animated sprites.
  • Tested with automated rule checks, many simulated full games, and browser tests on desktop and phone layouts covering every way a game can end.
